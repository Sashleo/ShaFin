How ShaFin works
  1. Add a company and choose its type: manufacturer, non-manufacturer or bank.
  2. Create an analysis and enter the current and prior year figures from the annual report, in the unit the report uses.
  3. Enter the assumptions the models need, each with its source and date. Nothing is filled in for you.
  4. Read the checks, ratios, models and flags, then generate a signed report.
